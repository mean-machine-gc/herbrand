Emits lend.book

The requested book must exist in the catalog
reads: book.exists
The requesting member must exist

late-fee-policy ⚙ llm
When a book is returned late, apply a late fee
Context lms
Activated by book.returned
Emits apply.late.fee
Actor AI assistant for catalog operations
View loan.due.date loan.returned.date

Preconditions

The loan must be past its due date
reads: loan.due.date, loan.returned.date

Operations

lend-operation ⚙ machine
Lend a book to a member
Context lms
Activated by lend.book
Actor Deterministic lending transaction processor

Constraints

Book must be available for lending
reads: book.available
Member must not be suspended
reads: member.suspended
Member must not have exceeded their loan limit
reads: member.active.loans, member.max.loans

Outcomes

book.lent
The book has been lent to the member
effects: book.available: Set to false member.active.loans: Incremented by 1 loan.due.date: Set to 14 days from now
if: Member has reached their loan limit after this loan
member.loan.limit.reached — Member has hit their borrowing limit
late-fee-operation ⚙ machine
Apply a late fee to a member
Context billing-service
Activated by apply.late.fee
Actor Deterministic fee calculation engine

Outcomes

late.fee.applied
A late fee has been charged
effects: member.balance: Debited by fee amount
